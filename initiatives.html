Bridge for Africa

Together with four other international students, I co-founded Bridge for Africa, a student-run project that pays school tuition for a student in Uganda. There's no big organization behind it: each of us contributes from our own pocket every month.

5student co-founders
500 TLcontributed by each member, every month
1student's tuition fully funded in Uganda

Our goal is to support more students and expand to other countries. If you'd like to contribute or get involved, I'd love to hear from you.
