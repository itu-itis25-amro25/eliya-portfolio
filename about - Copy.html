My story

Where I come from

My father is Polish and Lebanese, and my mother is Tanzanian, from Tunduru in the Ruvuma region. I spent my early childhood moving between Tanzania and Lebanon before settling back in Tanzania, and I grew up speaking English and Swahili. I take real pride in coming from cultures so different from one another. It taught me early on to adapt, listen, and see things from more than one side.

At 16, I won a scholarship to finish high school in Istanbul, Türkiye, and I've called this city home since 2021. I learned Turkish along the way and now speak it fluently. Today I live in Florya, Istanbul.

I joined a drone project with no programming or hardware experience. It sparked a passion that has stayed with me ever since.

In high school I joined a TÜBİTAK drone project. I came in with zero background and played a small part, but seeing mechanical and electrical parts come together into something that could actually fly changed how I saw technology. That's why I chose Computer Engineering, and why I want to work with innovative hardware components.

I graduated in 2025 with a 1540 SAT and a 108 TOEFL, and started my bachelor's at Istanbul Technical University the same year on the Türkiye Bursları merit scholarship.

I'd describe myself as introverted and empathetic: I'd rather listen first and understand a problem properly before I start solving it. That's also why I enjoy helping other students. I know how much the right advice at the right moment can change someone's path.
